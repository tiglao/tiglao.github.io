about

i'm a generalist in jamaica plain. i help groups own, build and run things together, and i cook and curate to tell stories and keep them.

i grew up in california, the kid of filipino immigrants, and studied neuroscience and art history at uc riverside. a neuroscience research job brought me to boston in 2010. i ran programs at east meets west bookstore, a community space for asian american artists, and started pamangan, a filipino pop up that hosted more than 200 dinners and gatherings. pamangan became tanám, a worker-owned restaurant in somerville where ten guests at a time sat down to multicourse filipino meals built around stories. it ran until 2023. i've spent more than twelve years in worker cooperatives, mostly in governance, facilitation and finances.
