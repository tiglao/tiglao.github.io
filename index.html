about

i'm an introvert who keeps setting big tables. i cook, i curate, and i help people own and run things together, mostly because we make more beautiful things when we're not alone.

i grew up in monterey, california, the eldest of filipino immigrants. i was not chill enough, so i made my way to boston, where i could be super extra about the things i love. these days most of my hours go to developing and implementing better ways to create together, and what i call organizational care work. i run a meeting with a spreadsheet open and grace lee boggs in mind. off the clock i'm prototyping something, lost in a long conversation, or watching anime. i work in boston and live in somerville.

some recent tables: pagkakaisa | solidaridad with lani asunción, mondays, and a rent party dinner for city life/vida urbana.
