july 19, 2025 · cloud & spirits, cambridge

pagkakaisa | solidaridad

transmuting imperialism's impact: a culinary and visual exchange. with lani asunción.

film by stephanie houten

about

a kamayan feast inside lani asunción's projections, with poets, dancers and djs.

kamayan is the filipinx practice of eating together by hand from banana leaves. lani and i paired it with projection mapping and live performance to look at how colonialism has shaped food, memory and identity across the filipinx diaspora and the communities we share space with.

forty guests came in two seatings of twenty. they ate filipinx dishes with diasporic lebanese and puerto rican influences while performers moved through the room and lani's duty-free paradise projections changed the space around them. a printed zine went home with every guest.
